summary: How integrated systems increase long-term reliability.

Integration is the quiet architecture of endurance. Each system we build
should strengthen the others without demanding their conformity.

Resilient design seeks harmony, not dominance.  It favors coordination over
control — allowing the whole to endure when any part must yield.

From water to energy to security, every domain carries the same moral
geometry: strength through balance, purpose through peace.
